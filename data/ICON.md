# Application icon artwork

Updated on 2026-11-10 as part of the original NextKde icon family. The
user-provided Notes reference establishes the rounded tile, soft vertical color,
ivory paper/symbols, fine edge and restrained depth. Symbols and category artwork
are drawn for NextKde rather than copied from macOS assets. The artwork remains
under this project's license.

Canonical sources:

- `nkmail.svg`

Qt SVG rendering and PNG/ICO exports were performed in the dedicated development
VM. The SVGs use self-contained paths and gradients without external images,
fonts or clipping paths, preserving transparent corners in Qt SVG. Catalog PNG
copies are maintained separately in `NextKde/appCatalog/icons` at 256 pixels.
Changing source artwork does not replace previously published application bundles.
